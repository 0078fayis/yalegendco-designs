Heavy enough to hold a boxy shape, soft enough for summer. Most high-street tees are 150 to 180.

No prints, no slogans. One plain woven label at the neck, flat seams, a slightly dropped shoulder.

Six tops, six bottoms, one palette. Any top goes with any bottom, which is the whole idea.

05 · How it is made

One workshop.
Small batches.

Every piece is knitted, dyed and sewn under one roof by a single workshop we know by name. We make 50 to 80 of each style. When they are gone, the next drop is a new colour, not a restock.
