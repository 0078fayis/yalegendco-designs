The story

Kora is the plain cloth before it becomes anything else.

In Malayalam, kora is unbleached, undyed cotton: the fabric at the start of everything. We named the label after it because that is what we wanted to sell. Good cloth, cut simply, with nothing on it.

The first drop is twelve pieces. Six tops, six bottoms, one palette. They were designed together so that any top works with any bottom, and so that you can buy two pieces this year and two more next year without anything looking out of place.

The fabric is a 280 gsm combed cotton jersey for the tees and crews, a 400 gsm loopback for the hoodies, and a 12 oz rigid denim and a brushed cotton twill for the bottoms. Everything is garment-washed before it is sewn, so there is no shrinkage after your first wash.

Tiruppur, in Tamil Nadu, makes a large share of the world's cotton knitwear. We work with one small unit there that knits, dyes and sews under one roof, pays above the state minimum, and lets us visit unannounced. We make 50 to 80 pieces per style. When a style sells out, it is replaced by a new colour rather than a restock.

Delivery

Free standard delivery anywhere in Australia, sent with tracking. Orders placed before 12 pm AEST leave the same business day. Metro addresses usually take 2 to 4 business days, regional 4 to 7. We do not ship outside Australia yet.

Returns

30 days from delivery, for any reason, as long as the piece is unworn and unwashed with the label attached. Start a return from your order email and we send a prepaid label. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of the piece arriving. Exchanges for another size are free.

Care

Cold machine wash inside out, with like colours. Line dry in the shade. Do not tumble dry, bleach or dry clean. A warm iron on the reverse is fine. The denim is rigid and will soften with wear; wash it as little as you can.

FAQ

Will you restock sold-out sizes?
Not in the same colour. Each style is made once, in a run of 50 to 80. The next drop brings new colours of the same cuts, so the fit stays the same.
Where is the clothing made?
Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, India, in one small knitwear unit that knits, dyes and sews under one roof. More in the story.
Is the model imagery real?
No. While we are a concept, the people in the photos are AI-generated. Garment photos of the real production run will replace them before launch.
Do you ship outside Australia?
Not yet. New Zealand is planned for the second drop.
How do I pay?
Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal at checkout. Prices include GST.
