Privacy

When you join the SelfStrata early list we collect your email address and the answers you choose in the form.

We use them only to tell you when SelfStrata launches and to understand what people need. We don't sell or share them, and we don't send marketing from anyone else.

To have your details deleted, reply to any email we send you and ask. We'll delete them within 7 days.
